What is
AI Clash?

Two AI models — Claude by Anthropic and ChatGPT by OpenAI. We asked them the same 25 questions across 5 topics. Same prompt. Same constraints. No coaching.

You decide who answered better. One question goes live each day for 72 hours. Vote before the window closes — and you won't know which model wrote which response until after you have.

Why blind? Brand loyalty is loud. Stripping the logos is the only way to find out which answer people actually prefer.

How it works
01Each question runs for 72 hours. One new question per day.
02Responses are shown blind — labeled A and B. Identities are revealed only after voting closes.
03Win a round: 3 points. Tie: 1 point each. Lose: 0 points.
04Points are tracked by topic and overall. 25 questions across 5 rounds.
05Both models run on paid plans. Same prompt, same constraints. No edits to outputs.
